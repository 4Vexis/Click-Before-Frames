# Click Before Frames

By **vVexis**.

An input assist for Geometry Dash 2.2081 on Android. If you click a little too early, the mod predicts what would happen and holds your click until it is safe. If your click was fine, it is never touched.

Use it for practice and fun, not for leaderboard or submitted runs.

## How to use

1. Tap the blue **V** button. It shows on every screen.
2. Pick a **mode**. **LOW**, **MED** and **HIGH** set how many ticks a click may be held back. **OFF** turns the whole mod off.
3. Under **Active in**, choose which gamemodes the mod works in.
4. **Drag** the V button anywhere. Its size is in the mod settings.
5. In a level, tap **V** on the pause menu to open the full settings without leaving the level.

## What to expect

- **Cube, Ball, Robot, Spider:** if you click in the air a little before landing or touching an orb, the mod simulates a hidden copy of your icon. If your normal click would kill you but a slightly later one would not, it holds the click until then. Clicks on the ground are never delayed.
- **Ship, UFO, Swing, Wave:** very short taps are stretched to a minimum length, so quick taps come out consistent.
- **Not supported yet:** dual mode and two-player.
- Portals or triggers just ahead of a click can confuse the prediction. This is an early version, so it may not help every time.

## Ticks

A tick is 1/240 of a second. 4 ticks is about 1 frame at 60 FPS. Smaller windows feel more natural and larger ones are more forgiving.

## Stats

The V menu shows live numbers: `clicks`, `air`, `safe`, `would-die` and `delayed`. If the mod seems to do nothing, play for a bit and check them. `would-die` staying at 0 means the prediction is not seeing deaths. Try the **Ghost counts as Player 1** setting.
